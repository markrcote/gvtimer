Privacy Policy for GVTimer
==========================

GVTimer runs entirely on your device and does not collect, store, or transmit any
personal data. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies,
and it does not connect to any external servers or online services.

Because GVTimer does not collect or process any personal information, there is no
data to share with third parties.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact pilcrowsoft@gmail.com.
